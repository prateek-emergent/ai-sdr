Figure 2 · one reply

How Giulia answers now

Audio is cleaned, written down in the call's language, and Claude answers with the customer card in mind; a switch hands the call to a twin agent in the other language.

Figure 3 · the loop

How she gets better

Every call is graded; people check the grader; simulated customers test a proposed prompt change; a person approves it before it ships to the pod.

In the call

  • Italian and English, with a handoff to a twin agent mid-call.
  • The customer card from HubSpot, prompt v4, an it/en parity check.
  • Speech-to-text in the call's language; Krisp noise filter.
  • A picked voice with a Deepgram backup; room tone, typing during lookups.
  • Each step's timing streamed live to the Talk page.

Around the call

  • Langfuse traces: masked, trimmed, public for test calls.
  • A grader on every call: 15 checks, 4 must-rules, scores to Langfuse.
  • Quality page: people check verdicts, the judge grades again.
  • 14 simulated customers and a propose-and-test step for prompt changes.

Measured on the pod

  • Reply in 1.2 to 1.5 s (median per call).
  • Voice audible about 0.1 s after the reply starts.
  • A 5-turn call costs 31 Langfuse units, down from 56.
  • About 9 s to grade a call; 45 tests pass.
